Krittin Chaowakarn

MSc Student at CTU in Prague · Researcher at IMPACT, CIIRC

chaowkri [at] cvut [dot] cz

Krittin is an MSc student in Open Informatics at the Czech Technical University in Prague. He also works as a researcher with the Intelligent Machine Perception (IMPACT) group at the Czech Institute of Informatics, Robotics and Cybernetics, with a focus on robot simulation.

Previously, he completed his B.Eng. in Electrical Engineering at Sirindhorn International Institute of Technology, Thammasat University, and spent a semester at the Technical University of Munich, where he conducted research on multi-object detection for autonomous vehicles.
